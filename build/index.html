03

How an order works

Three steps
1

You send the spec

Capacity, material, colour, neck size, quantity and delivery city. A sample bottle or a drawing helps most of all.

2

We confirm the bottle

We confirm whether we already have the mould. If not, we quote the mould first, before anything is committed.

3

We produce and deliver

We agree the quantity and the delivery, and confirm both in writing before the run starts.

04

What to send for a quote

Six things

Quotes come back faster when the first message has these in it.

  1. 01
    What the bottle is for: water, jars, cosmetic, pharmaceutical, oil, detergent, or something else
  2. 02
    Capacity, in millilitres or litres
  3. 03
    Material, if you know it: PET or HDPE
  4. 04
    Colour
  5. 05
    Neck size, or the cap you already use
  6. 06
    Quantity per order, and how often you would reorder

Not sure of the neck size? Send a photo of the cap and we will identify it.
