How ordering works

Three steps, in writing

Step one

Send the spec

Capacity, material, colour, quantity, and the cap you already use. A sample bottle or a photo helps most of all.

Step two

We confirm the mould

We already have it, or we quote a new mould before anything else is agreed.

Step three

Agreed before the run

Quantity and delivery are agreed in writing before the machines start.
