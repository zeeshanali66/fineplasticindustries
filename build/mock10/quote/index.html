Common questions

Can you make a shape you do not already run?

Yes. Send a sample bottle, a drawing, or a sketch with the capacity and the cap you use. We check whether an existing mould is close. If not, we quote the mould first, and it is agreed in writing before any bottle price.

Can you match a colour?

Yes. Colour is matched to your sample, and any shape can be made in your colour.

What plastics do you use?

PET and HDPE. Bottles, jars and jugs are blow-moulded; caps, closures and rigid parts are injection-moulded.

Do you supply caps as well as bottles?

Yes. Caps and closures are injection-moulded and quoted alongside the bottle, so one supplier covers both.
