About Fine PlasticIndustries.

A blow-moulding and injection-moulding factory in Lahore, making bottles for businesses that fill them and put their own label on.

What we do

PET and HDPE, blow-moulded and injection-moulded.

We make bottles, jars, jugs and closures. You fill them and put your own label on. Colour is matched to your sample. If we do not already have your mould, we quote one before anything else is agreed.

Who buys from us

  • Water and beverage
  • Cosmetic
  • Pharmaceutical
  • Edible oil
  • Detergent and household
  • Packaging traders
