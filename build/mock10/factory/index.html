The factory.

Blow moulding and injection moulding at Plot 53-B, Sundar Small Industrial Estate II, Sultanke, Lahore.

Blow moulding

A PET preform is heated and blown into a steel mould, taking its exact shape. HDPE bottles and jugs are blow-moulded too.

The same mould gives the same shape and weight on every run. The neck finish is set by the mould, so tell us which cap you already use and we match it.

Injection moulding

Caps, closures and rigid parts, injection-moulded and quoted alongside the bottle, so one supplier covers both.

Moulds and colour
